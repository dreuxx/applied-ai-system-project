# Data attribution

MTG-Jamendo Dataset by Dmitry Bogdanov, Minz Won, Philip Tovstogan, Alastair Porter and Xavier Serra (2019). Metadata copyright 2019–2023 Music Technology Group. This project joins title and artist metadata with automatic-tagging benchmark annotations, normalizes tag names, filters entries, adds instrument families and derives sparse vectors and genre indexes. The Jamendo metadata and these adaptations are provided under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for non-commercial academic/research use. Per-track Jamendo links identify the sources. No Jamendo audio is included. [Authors and usage terms](https://github.com/MTG/mtg-jamendo-dataset#license) · [Metadata license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) · [Hugging Face snapshot](https://huggingface.co/datasets/m-a-p/MTG/tree/e900ccbfcc7cc73c63e1e8ec85dbc00c0039f677/mtg-jamendo-dataset/data).

The Incompetech collection credits Kevin MacLeod. The 28 embedded MP3 previews are edited, seven-second mono excerpts. The individual titles, source recording links, excerpt offsets and license links are in [credits.csv](data/credits.csv). [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
